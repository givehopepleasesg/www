For someone facing illness, disability, injury, financial hardship or limited access to healthcare, the right support can change the direction of a life. GIVE HOPE PLEASE LIMITED exists to help turn compassion into practical action.

Who We Are

About Us

GIVE HOPE PLEASE LIMITED was founded on a simple belief: that no one should have to navigate life’s greatest challenges alone. Whether it is a family struggling to afford necessary medical treatments, a person living with a disability needing specialized care, or a student facing financial barriers, we step in to bridge the gap.

Vision

A compassionate world where every individual facing adversity receives the care, support, and opportunities needed to live with dignity and hope.

Mission

To empower communities and individuals by providing accessible healthcare assistance, rehabilitation support, educational opportunities, and holistic care through collaborative partnerships.

Our Name

GIVE: Active generosity.
HOPE: Restoring possibility.
PLEASE: Humility, respect, and heart behind our service.

Pillars of Care

What We Do

Healthcare & Medical Assistance

Subsidies and support for essential medical treatments, therapy sessions, and medical equipment for vulnerable individuals.

Rehabilitation & Therapy

Long-term physical therapy and rehabilitation programs to help patients regain mobility and independence after severe injury or illness.

Disability Support & Inclusion

Promoting accessibility, community integration, and tailored physical support for persons with disabilities (PWDs).

Elderly Care & Wellbeing

Focused healthcare and mobility support ensuring senior citizens age gracefully with comfort, dignity, and companionship.

Education & Scholarships

Supporting students through scholarships, educational assistance and skill development opportunities, particularly in the healthcare industry.

Outreach & Research

Community health screenings, preventive education, and collaborative initiatives to strengthen health equity.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to apply for assistance?

Individuals facing illness, disability, injury, or financial hardship who require healthcare, therapy, or living support can apply.

How are donation funds utilized?

Donations go directly toward funding medical equipment, rehabilitation subsidies, elderly care, and educational grants for beneficiaries.

Can healthcare institutions refer patients?

Yes, we welcome clinical referrals from hospitals, clinics, and social service agencies via our partner pathway.
